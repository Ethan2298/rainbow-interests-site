Internal integration

Disconnect QuickBooks

Intuit uses this page as the post-revoke landing. Opening it directly does not disconnect a QuickBooks company.

If Intuit sent you here

After a successful revoke in Intuit, the QuickBooks connection for this app is terminated. The public Rainbow Interests website does not keep QuickBooks records or connection tokens. An authorized administrator should also remove the matching local CLI profile so stored tokens are deleted from the device.

Disconnect from QuickBooks

To revoke access, use the app-connection settings in QuickBooks, or contact Rainbow Interests and identify the company that should be disconnected. Visiting this URL by itself does not revoke access.

Connect or reconnect

If you still need access, or a disconnect was unintentional, use the connect or reconnect page. Connection is available only to authorized Rainbow Interests personnel and authorized administrators of the QuickBooks company. Each company must be approved separately through Intuit's authorization screen.
