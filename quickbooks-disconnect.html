Internal integration

Disconnect QuickBooks

A QuickBooks company administrator can revoke access at any time.

Revoke access

Use the app-connection settings in QuickBooks to revoke the Rainbow Interests connection, or contact Rainbow Interests and identify the company that should be disconnected.

Local removal

After revocation, an authorized Rainbow Interests administrator will remove the matching local CLI profile and its stored connection tokens.
