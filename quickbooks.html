Internal integration

QuickBooks Integration

Rainbow Interests uses the official Intuit CLI to support authorized accounting and reporting work across separately approved QuickBooks companies.

Private internal use

This integration is not a public software service. It is available only to personnel authorized by Rainbow Interests and by the QuickBooks company being accessed.

Separate company authorization

Every QuickBooks company must grant access through Intuit's authorization screen. Permission for one company does not grant access to another company.

Connection security

The official Intuit CLI stores connection tokens on the authorized user's local device using protected local storage and the operating system keychain. The public Rainbow Interests website does not intentionally store QuickBooks records or connection tokens.

Support

For questions, connection help, or a request to disconnect, contact Rainbow Interests through the public contact information below.
