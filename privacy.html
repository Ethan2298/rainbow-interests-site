Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Rainbow Interests, LLC handles information collected through this website.

Effective date: July 30, 2026

Scope

Rainbow Interests, LLC is a holding company. This policy applies to information collected through this website. It does not govern the privacy practices of independently operated businesses, franchise locations, or third-party websites that may be linked from this site.

Information we collect

We do not require visitors to create an account, and this website does not collect or process contact-form submissions. If you choose to contact us through the public contact details published on this site, we may receive the information you choose to share, such as your name, contact details, and message.

How we use information

We may use information you voluntarily provide to respond to an inquiry, operate and improve this website, protect the site, and comply with applicable law. We do not sell personal information, and this website does not send automated text messages.

Limited website analytics

We use Vercel Web Analytics to understand aggregate page traffic and improve this website. Vercel describes this service as cookie-free and designed to provide anonymized, aggregate analytics. The service may collect limited technical and usage information, such as pages viewed, referrer, device and browser type, and general geographic information. We do not intentionally send names, email addresses, or other sensitive information to analytics.

Sharing

We may share information with service providers that help us operate this website, including Vercel for hosting and analytics, and when required by law or necessary to protect rights, safety, or security. We do not share information for cross-site behavioral advertising.

Data retention and security

We retain information only as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, legal obligations, or dispute resolution. No internet transmission or storage system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your choices

You may choose not to contact us or not to provide requested information. Depending on where you live, you may have privacy rights concerning personal information we hold about you. To make a request or ask a privacy question, please use the public contact method available on this website.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date above will indicate when it was last revised.
