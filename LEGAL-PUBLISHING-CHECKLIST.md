# Legal Pages Publishing Checklist

- [ ] Confirm the company’s organizing state, governing-law, and venue language in the Terms of Service.
- [ ] Confirm current website data practices, including that Vercel Web Analytics is the only analytics service enabled and that no sensitive query parameters or custom events are sent.
- [ ] Confirm that the QuickBooks integration is limited to authorized internal users and that no QuickBooks records or OAuth tokens are stored by the public website.
- [ ] Confirm the Vercel request-log retention that applies to the OAuth callback page.
- [ ] Confirm that the Intuit production application uses the privacy, terms, connect, disconnect, launch, and callback URLs documented in `INTUIT-PRODUCTION-SETUP.md`.
- [ ] Have the final pages reviewed by qualified counsel for the jurisdictions where the site is offered.
